Add Dialog: a composite Component on the Radix primitive with Root, Trigger, Content, Title, Description, and Close parts. Opening it moves focus into the panel and holds it there, the page behind stops scrolling and leaves the accessibility tree, and Escape, a click on the overlay, and the close button each close it and return focus to the trigger. Controlled or uncontrolled, with open and close animations that drop out under reduced motion.
