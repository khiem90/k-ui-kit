Add Switch: a labelled on/off switch that announces as a switch, toggles with Space and Enter, and works controlled or uncontrolled. Inside a form, a hidden checkbox carries its name and value, honours `required`, and follows a form reset. The track is a pill: cream with a 2px edge and a muted thumb when off, Ember with a cream thumb when on.
