Add Select: a composite Component on the Radix primitive with Root, Trigger, Content, Item, and Group parts. The trigger shows the picked option or a placeholder, in the three TextField sizes. Enter, Space, and the arrow keys open the list, arrows move through the options and skip disabled ones, typing jumps to a match, Enter picks, and Escape closes and returns focus to the trigger. A long list scrolls, with a button at either end. Controlled or uncontrolled, and inside a form it submits its value under the name it is given.
